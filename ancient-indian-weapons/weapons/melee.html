Melee Arsenal

Close Quarters Combat

Melee weapons were the backbone of the Padati (infantry) and the primary tool for close-quarters skirmishes and duels. Engineered for devastating shock value, armor-piercing capabilities, and agile slashing, these weapons represent the pinnacle of ancient Indian metallurgical design.

Khadga

Heavy straight sword utilizing Wootz steel for devastating slashing power.

Katar

Armor-piercing push dagger, essential for close-quarters infantry melees.

Urumi

Flexible whip-sword, a masterclass in spring-steel metallurgy.

Gada

Heavy blunt mace engineered to crush heavy cavalry armor.
